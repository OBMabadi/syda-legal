Privacy Policy

What we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Overview

SYDA is a personal activity journal for sharing your daily activities with friends or publicly. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, where it is stored, and the choices you have. We built SYDA with privacy in mind and we do not sell your personal information.

Information We Collect

Account information: when you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive a user identifier and, if you choose to share them, your name and email address. Profile information: your display name, username, bio, profile photo, and optional details such as gender, birth date, and location. Content you create: posts, photos, star ratings, tags, reactions, comments, and private messages. Usage information needed to operate and sync the app.

Location

Location is optional and off by default. If you enable the "show location" setting, SYDA requests "When In Use" permission with reduced accuracy and converts the result into a city and country using Apple's system services. We never store or display raw coordinates — only the city and country are shown, and only if you choose to share them.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to create and manage your account, store and sync your content across your devices, show your content to the people you allow, help keep the community safe through blocking and reporting, and comply with legal requirements. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored using Apple CloudKit in a private database tied to your iCloud account. Syncing between your devices is handled by Apple's CloudKit service. This means Apple's own privacy and security terms also apply to how your data is transmitted and stored.

Visibility and Sharing

You control who sees your profile through your account visibility setting: Public, Friends only, or Private. You can also choose, field by field, which parts of your profile (such as your photo, name, bio, gender, birth date, or location) are shown to others. Content you post is shared according to the visibility you select.

Third-Party Sign-In

Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google are provided by Apple and Google. When you use them, your use of those services is governed by their respective privacy policies. SYDA only receives the basic account information described above.

Children's Privacy

SYDA is not intended for anyone under 13 years old. We do not knowingly allow children under 13 to create an account, and eligibility is checked at first sign-up using Apple's Declared Age Range API. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us so we can remove it.

Your Choices and Rights

You can edit or delete your content, adjust your profile and field visibility, block other users, and turn notifications on or off at any time. You can delete your entire account from Settings, which permanently removes your account and the content associated with it. For any other request about your data, use the Contact us form on the About screen.

Data Retention

Your content remains available until you delete it or delete your account. When you delete your account, we remove your account and its associated data as part of a single operation. Some information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate safety reasons.

Security

We rely on Apple's platform security, including iCloud and CloudKit, to protect your data in transit and at rest. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep access to your sign-in provider and device protected.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the date shown at the end of this page. We encourage you to review it periodically.

Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this policy or your data, you can reach us through the Contact us form on the About screen.
